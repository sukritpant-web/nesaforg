Service Area

Baseline

Are you planning to undertake the study that serves as a reference point, against which the progress, impact, or outcomes of the project or program can be measured? By conducting a baseline study, you ensure that your program or project is both data-driven and results-oriented.

Evaluation

We provide expertise in evaluation – before, during, or after implementation of the program or project. We support to determine whether the program has achieved its goals, identify areas for improvement, and provide evidence-based information that can guide decision-making. Your interest area - processes, outcomes, or impacts evaluation?

Tool Testing

Do you intend to assess a tool to make sure it fulfills the goals for which it was created, operates as intended, and performs as intended? We are here to support you to test the tool to ensure quality, performance, and user satisfaction.

Data Analysis

We give help to transform your raw data making meaning out of it. We specialize in usage of diversity of statistical and analytical approaches to transform data into insights.

Program Project Implementation

We are expanding our area and implementing new projects. Our team values the interconnectedness of project execution, ensuring resources and members align to meet goals. Successful implementation demands careful monitoring, adaptability, effective collaboration, coordination, cooperation, and communication (the 4Cs) with stakeholders.

Market System Development

We offer market research, rapid market analysis, identification and resolution of market failures, and improvement of market actors' access and capacity. We implement Market System Development (MSD) and Participatory Market System Development (PMSD) in rural and urban areas to address systemic challenges. Our approach supports the development of inclusive and sustainable market systems by fostering partnerships, enhancing the business environment, and creating opportunities for marginalized groups.

Livelihood and Value Chain

We focus on enhancing livelihoods through value chain analysis, training on value chain marketing, private sector engagement and market development. We prioritize value chain marketing by identifying market opportunities, understanding customer needs, fostering relationships, enhancing product value, improving market access, and to increase economic opportunities to drive competitiveness and sustainable growth.
